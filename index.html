Chapter 5 · Payment and risk
Wed 25 Nov · 9:10 pm

He pays in one tap. Behind the scenes, every order is read before it is made.

A personalised album that comes back cannot be resold, so one returned order costs you the shipping and the product. Your site offers partial COD to protect against this. The network can tell which orders are likely to come back before you start making them.

Without Fastrr (public pages today)
  • Partial COD with an advance up front.
  • Two pages word it differently: "50% upfront" and "₹500 in advance".
With Fastrr
  • QuickPay and payment personalisation.
  • RTO risk tags before the order is made.
  • Pay After Delivery for hesitant shoppers.

RTO risk tags

Scored from pincode, address and buyer history against real delivery outcomes, so a risky first-time COD order can be asked to prepay before you make it.

Pay After Delivery

A hesitant shopper approves with UPI and is charged only after delivery.

Chapter 6 · After
Thu 26 Nov onwards

The date he saw is the date he gets, and a missed delivery is caught early.

After the order, Karan uploads his photos through your SMS link and waits. The promise he saw at checkout is what he measures everything against, and a failed delivery the day before an anniversary is the worst possible outcome.

Without Fastrr (public pages today)
  • Photos uploaded through an SMS link or the "Send Photos" page.
  • Tracking links on lovingcrafts.org.
With Fastrr
  • The same delivery date, tracked through the network.
  • Failed-delivery (NDR) tags so a bad address or an absent buyer is flagged early.
  • One-tap reorder next occasion through SSO.

NDR tags

When a delivery attempt fails, the reason is tagged and acted on quickly, so the gift is not sent back a day before the occasion.

Reorder with SSO

For Meera's birthday in March, Karan signs in with one tap and his address is already there.
